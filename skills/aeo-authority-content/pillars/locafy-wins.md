# Pillar — Locafy / Partner Wins (Case Studies)

Real results, real numbers, real businesses. The go-to-market reality is that **Locafy's partners sell Locafy, not Jason directly.** Partner wins are the hero — Locafy is the engine.

## Primary Data Hooks

| Signal | Source | Post angle |
|---|---|---|
| Partner revenue/lead result | Jason shares in chat / ClickUp note | "Partner drove $X in Y months. Here's the playbook." |
| GSC 90-day transformation | `gsc-performance-history` | "A partner's traffic went from X to Y in 90 days. The 4 moves:" |
| First AI-citation on partner brand | `brand-radar-ai-responses` | "A partner's brand just got recommended by ChatGPT for the first time." |
| Multi-keyword breakout | `gsc-keywords` (multiple top-20 new entries) | "Partner ranked for 12 new top-20 keywords this month. The pattern:" |

## Hard Rules

1. **Anonymize by default.** "A roofing partner in the Southeast" > "RoofCo LLC". Named partners only with explicit Jason clearance.
2. **Round hard-to-hide numbers.** If $612,438 identifies the partner, round to "$600K+".
3. **Partner is the hero.** The post should read "partner X won this", not "Locafy did this to partner X."
4. **Locafy is the engine, mentioned once.** One sentence. No product pitch.
5. **No hard sell.** Case studies end with insight, not "DM me to learn more."

## Post Angles

- "A roofing partner generated $600K+ in new business in 5 months. No paid ads. Here's what they did:"
- "A Southeast HVAC operator went from 0 AI citations to 8 in 60 days. The shift, in 4 parts:"
- "Before / after: a plumbing partner's search visibility after 6 months with the platform."
- "Partners running the Localizer playbook are getting cited in AI answers. Here's what that looks like:"

## Hook Library

- "[$number] in [window]. One [archetype] partner. Here's what we built."
- "A [archetype] company had [old state]. Six months later: [new state]. The playbook:"
- "Ranked #1 is yesterday's goal. Being cited is today's. This partner did both in 90 days."

## Anti-Patterns

- Leading with Locafy. Lead with the partner's outcome.
- Hero-worship of Locafy ("we changed everything"). Modesty scales; bravado doesn't.
- Publishing results that could trace back to a specific client without sign-off.
- Vague numbers ("significant growth") — always a real figure.

## Creative Default

- **Big number** over a clean background ("$600K in 5 months").
- Or a **before/after bar chart** (traffic, citations, keyword count).
- Anonymized screenshots when the visual is GSC / Ahrefs data.

## Clearance Checklist

Before drafting anything identifying:
- [ ] Specific partner named? → Jason sign-off required.
- [ ] Exact revenue figure? → Round or Jason sign-off.
- [ ] Screenshot shows URL / brand? → Crop or blur, or sign-off.
- [ ] Geography narrow enough to identify? → Broaden ("Southeast" instead of "Atlanta").

Default: assume you can't name them.
