# Pillar — AI Search Updates (Business Impact)

React to platform-level changes (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini) — but **always back the reaction with own-data**.

## The Rule

No news post ships without at least one number from Jason's own tracked properties. If there's no own-data angle, skip. Plenty of people write "ChatGPT has a new feature" — Jason's edge is "here's what that looks like in my data."

## Primary Sources for News

- Google Search Central Blog (developers.google.com/search/blog)
- OpenAI blog, Perplexity blog
- Search Engine Roundtable / Search Engine Land / Search Engine Journal
- TechCrunch / The Verge for major product announcements

Use `web_search` inside the skill when drafting.

## Pairing News with Data

For every platform update, pair it with one of:

| News event | Paired own-data |
|---|---|
| Google AI Overviews expanded | GSC impressions on affected query set (before/after) |
| ChatGPT rolls out search | Brand Radar AI-responses count for tracked brands |
| Perplexity citation changes | Brand Radar cited-pages delta |
| Google core update | GSC position-history for a representative keyword |

If the paired data doesn't move meaningfully, you don't have a post. Don't force it.

## Post Angles

- "Google just [change]. Here's what it did to [partner archetype]'s organic traffic in 7 days."
- "ChatGPT Search rolled out. Here's what AI citations looked like for a roofing client before vs after."
- "Perplexity changed how it cites. Our Brand Radar shows [specific delta]."
- "The [algorithm update] is rolling out. Here's the actual position impact on a [niche] keyword:"

## Hook Library

- "Google just moved your organic result one screen lower. Here's what that costs [niche]."
- "[Platform] shipped [feature] on [date]. Here's what it did to my data by [date + 7]."
- "Most people react to AI search news with opinions. Here's what the numbers say."

## Anti-Patterns

- Reacting to news >5 days old as if it's breaking.
- Opinion-only posts (no data = no post in this pillar).
- "This changes everything" hype.
- Repeating what Search Engine Land already said without adding your own angle.

## Creative Default

- **Split-view** (news screenshot + data chart) is ideal.
- Or **one clear metric** callout over a branded background.
