# Pillar — AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

Educate the market on AEO. Jason is a leader here; the goal is to own the vocabulary and frame the shift.

## Primary Data Hooks

Best evidence comes from Brand Radar. The post is strongest when it cites **what AI actually said** about a tracked brand.

| Signal | Query | Post angle |
|---|---|---|
| New AI citation on a tracked brand | `brand-radar-ai-responses` | "ChatGPT recommended [client type] today. Here's the exact answer." |
| SOV jump vs competitors | `brand-radar-sov-history` | "Share of voice in [space] just shifted — here's what moved." |
| Cited-pages change | `brand-radar-cited-pages` | "This page now gets cited by AI. Here's why." |
| Brand impressions climbing | `brand-radar-impressions-history` | "AI is sending [N]% more impressions this quarter." |

## Post Angles (from `locafy-linkedin`, re-anchored to data)

- "AEO is not SEO. Here's the difference and why it matters now — backed by [specific data pull]."
- "How to get your business recommended by ChatGPT. Here's what the answer looks like when it works:"
- "The shift from ranking to being cited — [partner] went from 0 AI citations to [N] in [window]."

## What Makes an AEO Post Land

- **A real AI-answer screenshot** (anonymize partner brand if not cleared).
- **A concrete definition** — don't assume the reader knows AEO.
- **A practical diagnostic** — "here's how to check your current AI citations."
- **No hype.** "AI is eating search" without numbers is filler.

## Hook Library

- "A roofing company just got recommended by ChatGPT. They didn't know until we showed them."
- "Being ranked #1 on Google used to be the goal. Now it's being cited in the answer."
- "I asked ChatGPT which [service] company to hire in [city]. Here's who came up — and why."

## Anti-Patterns

- "AI is the future of search" — no data, no point.
- "Everyone needs AEO now" — be specific about who and why.
- Using AEO as a buzzword without defining it in the post.

## Creative Default

- **Screenshot of the AI answer** (cropped + anonymized where needed).
- Or a **Brand Radar chart** (use `render-time-series-chart` on SOV / mentions history).
